# Insight Framework

**Insight = observation + so-what + evidence + confidence + action.**

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| High | stable across days, both consistent reads and sensitivity variants |
| Medium | depends on an OPEN assumption or a noisy subset |
| Low | single day / few samples / contradicted by the other side |

Template sentence: "[Sites/cluster] explain [x] pts of lost availability ([period]); [evidence]; recommend [action]."
