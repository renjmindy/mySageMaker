# Handler test capacity, September-October 2026 - Executive Summary

**Bottom line**: About 91% of front and 96% of rear test positions were able to test, or 86% and 91% if a question about 40 positions is answered the other way; we recommend inspecting two front groups first.

## Why
1. **Where the front loss is.** Two groups of eight neighbouring positions are out of service most of the time, and a newer group of five has joined them. They cost about 6 percentage points of front capacity last week.
2. **The rear is smaller.** A group of four positions is out of service now and a newer group of five broke recently; together they cost about 2 points last week.
3. **A question about 40 positions.** Since 5 October the machine reports about 40 more closed positions than it names. We do not know whether they are closed on purpose or the list is cut off; that is the difference between 90% and 77% of front positions able to test.
4. **No clear trend.** Differences under 1.5 points are within normal noise.

## Confidence and limits
- Plausible range: front 90.3% to 91.4%, rear 95.5% to 96.5%. Counting the 40 positions as closed gives 86.2% and 91.5%.
- OEE cannot be reported yet: it needs run and idle time, output, good-unit and cycle-time data, which do not exist. The figures above show only positions able to test.
- About 3% of readings were unreadable and excluded.
- Neighbouring positions failing together suggests a shared part, such as a board; this is a hypothesis, not confirmed.
