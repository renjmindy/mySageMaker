# Handler test capacity, September-October 2026 - Executive Summary

**Bottom line**: On average 91% of front test positions and 96% of rear positions were able to test; three groups of front positions are still out of service, so we recommend inspecting them first.

## Why
1. **Where the front loss is.** Three groups of neighbouring positions are out of service most of the time and still were last week. Together they cost about 7 percentage points of front capacity in the last 7 days.
2. **The rear is smaller and changes.** Rear positions that failed earlier are only partly out now, about 30% of the time. One group broke recently and cost about 1.5 points last week.
3. **No clear trend.** Capacity did not clearly improve or worsen. Day-to-day swings of about 2 points are normal; differences under 1.5 points are within normal noise.

## Confidence and limits
- Plausible range for the month: front between 90.6% and 92.1%, rear between 95.3% and 96.8%. Front could be as low as 90.9%, because the screen reading sometimes misses closed positions.
- OEE (overall equipment effectiveness) cannot be reported yet. It needs run and idle time, output, good-unit and cycle-time data, which do not exist yet. The figures above show only how many positions can test.
- About 3% of screen readings were unreadable and excluded.
- Neighbouring positions failing together suggests a shared part, such as a board. This is a hypothesis, not confirmed. No maintenance records exist yet to check repairs.
