# Methodology Explanation Patterns

Worked example: a 4-minute reading shows 22 closed sites -> 234 of 256 open (91.4%). The day's availability is the average of such readings, each weighted by its duration. A reading is assumed to hold until the next one (at most 15 minutes), so a failed reading does not get its own weight - the previous valid state is carried across it.

Layered explanation: (1) one sentence, (2) four cleaning bullets, (3) formula, (4) limitations, (5) reproduction commands.
