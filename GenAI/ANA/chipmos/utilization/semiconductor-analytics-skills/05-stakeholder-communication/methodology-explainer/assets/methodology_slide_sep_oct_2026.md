# How we measured capacity

- **Data**: the handler's screen, read about every 4 minutes, for front positions 1-256 and rear 257-512, 1 September to 21 October 2026.
- **Measure**: the share of the 256 positions per side able to test, averaged over time. Each reading counts until the next one, at most 15 minutes.
- **Two choices that move the number**: leaving out clearly failed readings (counts of 252 to 256) raises the front figure by 0.77 points, and weighting by duration moves it about 0.65 points (rules A8, A18).
- **How sure**: plausible range front 90.3-91.4%, rear 95.5-96.5%. Since 5 October about 40 more closed positions are reported than named; counting them gives front 86.2%, rear 91.5%. This is not OEE, which cannot be reported yet.
