# How we measured capacity

- **Data**: the handler's screen, read about every 4 minutes, for front positions 1-256 and rear 257-512, 1 September to 2 October 2026.
- **Measure**: the share of the 256 positions per side able to test, averaged over time. Each reading counts for the time until the next one, at most 15 minutes.
- **Two choices that move the number**: leaving out clearly failed readings (counts of 252 to 256) raises the front figure by 1.3 points, and weighting by duration moves it about 0.65 points (rules A8, A18).
- **How sure**: a plausible range (front 90.6-92.1%, rear 95.3-96.8%) and at least this much (front 90.9%, rear 95.6%). This is not OEE, which cannot be reported yet.
