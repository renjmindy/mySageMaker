# Peer Review Framework

## Who and what
- The reviewer holds the **reviewer** role in the stakeholder map and is **not the author**. The process owner names the person. A review by the author, or by a reader who did not reproduce the numbers, does not count.
- The review is of a **commit**: the record names it, and it must be part of the repository's history. The author prepares the claims to reproduce (generated by `scripts/review_prep.py`); the reviewer reproduces them with `scripts/independent_recompute.py` and the pipeline.
- The reviewer needs access to the private data folder (`dat/`) and about a minute for the pipeline. Without the data a reviewer can check the documents and the code but not the reproduction, and must record the reproduction as not done.

## Severity
| Severity | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| blocking | the release must not go out until it is fixed and the reviewer has re-checked it | a wrong definition or scope; a number that does not reproduce (outside 0.01 percentage points); an over-claim (an OEE figure, a money figure, a physical cause stated as fact); a document that contradicts the code; an unconfirmed rule presented as confirmed; private data in the change |
| non-blocking | worth fixing, does not change what the reader concludes | naming, chart polish, an extra sensitivity table |
| note | an observation with no requested change | a suggestion for later |

## Findings and responses
Each finding has an id, severity, evidence (a command, file or number), a requested change and a status: **open**, **fixed**, **disputed** or **verified** (the reviewer re-checked it). The author answers in the response record: fixed (with the change made and the commit or file), disputed (with the reason) or deferred (with an action). A finding is only closed by the reviewer marking it verified.

## Verdict
- `pending` - not reviewed yet or review in progress.
- `approve` - no open finding of any severity.
- `approve with changes` - no open blocking finding; non-blocking findings may remain open.
- `reject` - at least one blocking finding is not verified.
After the author responds and the reviewer re-checks, the verdict is updated; the record keeps the history in the findings table.

## What this framework does not set
No response-time rule has been agreed (the plan has no deadlines yet). The process owner and the reviewer set one when the reviewer is named.
