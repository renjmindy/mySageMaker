What changes

- rag_client.py: uses the websockets library, not httpx. Maintains one long-lived connection (reconnect with exponential backoff, heartbeat ping every 30s).
- Concurrency: multiplex requests over the single WS with a per-request request_id field, so parallel user chats share the connection. A dict {request_id: asyncio.Queue} routes incoming frames back to the right caller.
- Lifecycle: open the WS in FastAPI's lifespan startup, close on shutdown.
- Message framing (assumed, swap when you share DGX's):
  - Client → DGX: {"type":"query","request_id":"...","prompt":"...","history":[...]}
  - DGX → Client: {"type":"token","request_id":"...","text":"..."}, {"type":"citation","request_id":"...","source":"...","page":...}, {"type":"done","request_id":"..."}
- Browser leg stays SSE — simpler than WS for one-way token streaming, and no change to the public API.

Everything else (schema, routes, repo layout, auth) is unchanged.
